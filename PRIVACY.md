# Privacy information

This document describes the intended user-facing data flows of the current development-stage AgentOffice desktop application. It is not a comprehensive legal privacy policy or a guarantee about an unreleased build. A release-specific privacy review is required before public distribution.

- Workspace and task information in the current desktop design is stored locally. Protect your device and back up important local data.
- Account authentication may communicate with the configured authentication service, and Google or GitHub sign-in involves the selected identity provider. These services have their own privacy notices.
- Update checks and downloads contact the official release hosting service. The service may process connection and request information under its own terms.
- Diagnostic information may exist on the device. Do not attach unreviewed diagnostic files or sensitive workspace data to public support reports.
- Planned AI features are not yet connected. Any future service that sends workspace content to an external AI provider requires a separate review and user-facing disclosure.

For privacy questions, request a private contact through [Support](SUPPORT.md) without posting personal data. Before a public release, the maintainers must confirm actual data flows, retention, deletion options, provider disclosures and an appropriate privacy contact.
